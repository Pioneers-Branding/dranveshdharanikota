Advanced imaging technique using fluorescent dye for real-time visualization of blood flow, lymphatics, and tumor margins during surgery.

Clinical Applications

Diverse applications of ICG fluorescence in oncological surgery

Sentinel Lymph Node Mapping

Real-time identification of sentinel lymph nodes for accurate staging in breast, gynecologic, and other cancers.

Procedures

  • Breast Cancer Staging
  • Gynecologic Cancer Evaluation
  • Melanoma Assessment
  • Gastrointestinal Cancer Staging

Key Benefits

Precise lymph node identification with minimal false negative rates

Anastomotic Assessment

Evaluation of blood supply to anastomotic sites to ensure adequate perfusion and reduce leak rates.

Procedures

  • Colorectal Anastomosis
  • Esophageal Anastomosis
  • Small Bowel Anastomosis

Key Benefits

Reduced anastomotic leak rates and improved surgical outcomes

Tumor Localization

Enhanced visualization of tumor margins and residual disease for complete surgical resection.

Procedures

  • Lung Nodules

Technical Specifications

Understanding the science behind ICG fluorescence imaging

Wavelength

Near-infrared light (700-900nm) for optimal tissue penetration

Dosage

0.25-0.5 mg/kg body weight, administered intravenously

Timing

Visualization within minutes to hours depending on application

Equipment

Specialized near-infrared imaging systems integrated with surgical platforms
