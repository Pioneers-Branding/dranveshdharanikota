About Dr. Anvesh Dharanikota

Senior Consultant Surgical Oncologist & Head of Department Laparoscopic & Robotic Surgeon
Well accomplished Surgical Oncologist with 12+ years of surgical experience in minimally invasive and robotic-assisted oncological surgeries. Recognized as a skillful surgeon specializing in complex gastrointestinal surgeries and breast oncoplastic surgeries.

Key Areas of Expertise

Specialized skills and advanced techniques in surgical oncology and cancer care

Minimally Invasive & Robotic Surgery

Advanced robotic and laparoscopic techniques for GI & Gynecological malignancies using Da Vinci system

Oncoplastic Breast Surgery

Specialized breast cancer treatment combining oncological excellence with aesthetic preservation

CRS & HIPEC Procedures

Complex cytoreductive surgery with heated chemotherapy for peritoneal surface malignancies

Multidisciplinary Care Coordination

Tumor boards leadership and collaborative approach with specialists for comprehensive treatment
