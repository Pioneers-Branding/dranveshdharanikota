GI & Thoracic Oncology

Comprehensive surgical care for cancers of the digestive system and chest, using advanced minimally invasive techniques.

Understanding GI & Thoracic Cancers

Gastrointestinal (GI) and thoracic oncology focuses on cancers affecting the digestive tract and organs within the chest cavity. This includes cancers of the esophagus, stomach, colon, rectum, liver, pancreas, and lungs.

Early and accurate diagnosis is critical. Treatment often involves a multidisciplinary approach, with surgery playing a key role in removing tumors and achieving long-term control.

Recognizing the Signs

Symptoms can be subtle. Awareness of these signs is crucial for early detection.

Common Symptoms

Difficulty swallowing or persistent indigestion.
Unexplained weight loss or loss of appetite.
Changes in bowel habits (diarrhea, constipation, blood in stool).
Persistent abdominal pain, bloating, or cramps.
Chronic cough, chest pain, or shortness of breath.

Advanced Surgical Procedures

Dr. Anvesh specializes in minimally invasive and robotic procedures for GI and thoracic cancers.

Laparoscopic & Robotic Colectomy

Minimally invasive removal of parts of the colon for colorectal cancer.

VATS Lobectomy

Video-assisted thoracoscopic surgery to remove a lobe of the lung for lung cancer.

Robotic Esophagectomy/Gastrectomy

Precise removal of the esophagus or stomach using the Da Vinci robotic system.

The Surgical Journey

A structured and supportive approach from diagnosis to recovery.

Comprehensive Diagnosis

Utilizing advanced imaging and endoscopy to accurately stage the cancer and plan the surgical approach.

Personalized Surgical Plan

Designing a tailored surgical strategy, choosing between robotic, laparoscopic, or open surgery for the best outcome.

Precision Surgery

Performing the procedure with a focus on complete tumor removal, preservation of function, and minimal tissue damage.

Post-Operative Recovery

A structured recovery plan including nutritional support and physical therapy to ensure a smooth return to health.
