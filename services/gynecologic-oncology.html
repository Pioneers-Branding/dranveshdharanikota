Gynecologic Oncology

Comprehensive and compassionate surgical care for women with cancers of the reproductive system.

Understanding Gynecologic Cancers

Gynecologic oncology deals with cancers of the female reproductive organs, including the uterus (endometrial cancer), ovaries, cervix, vulva, and vagina. Treatment requires specialized surgical expertise.

Dr. Anvesh is highly skilled in performing complex gynecologic cancer surgeries using minimally invasive robotic and laparoscopic techniques, which are particularly beneficial for procedures within the pelvis.

Recognizing the Signs

Awareness of symptoms is key to early detection and successful treatment.

Key Symptoms

Abnormal vaginal bleeding or discharge.
Pelvic pain or pressure.
Abdominal or back pain.
Bloating, difficulty eating, or feeling full quickly.
Changes in bathroom habits (urinary or bowel).

Surgical Procedures

Dr. Anvesh offers a full spectrum of advanced surgical treatments for gynecologic cancers.

Robotic Hysterectomy

Minimally invasive removal of the uterus for endometrial or cervical cancer.

Cytoreductive Surgery for Ovarian Cancer

A complex procedure to remove as much cancerous tissue as possible, often combined with HIPEC.

The Surgical Journey

A supportive and empowering path to treatment and recovery.

Comprehensive Evaluation

Detailed pelvic exams, advanced imaging (MRI/CT), and biopsies to accurately diagnose and stage the cancer.

Personalized Surgical Strategy

Developing a tailored surgical plan that may include fertility-sparing options for eligible patients.

Minimally Invasive Surgery

Performing robotic or laparoscopic hysterectomy, oophorectomy, or lymphadenectomy for precision and fast recovery.

Holistic Post-Operative Care

Focus on managing recovery, hormonal changes, and providing emotional support throughout the healing process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Robotic surgery for cancers like uterine and cervical cancer allows for highly precise movements in the narrow pelvic area. This leads to less blood loss, shorter hospital stays, faster recovery, and better preservation of nerves.

In some cases of early-stage cervical or ovarian cancer, fertility-sparing surgery may be an option. This is a highly individualized decision that Dr. Anvesh will discuss with you based on your cancer type, stage, and personal wishes.

A radical hysterectomy is the removal of the uterus, cervix, upper part of the vagina, and surrounding tissues (parametrium). It is a primary treatment for cervical cancer and is often performed robotically by Dr. Anvesh for a minimally invasive approach.
