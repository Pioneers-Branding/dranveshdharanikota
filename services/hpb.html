HPB (Hepato-Pancreato-Biliary) Surgery

Specialized surgical treatment for complex cancers of the liver, pancreas, gallbladder, and bile ducts.

Understanding HPB Cancers

HPB cancers are among the most challenging to treat due to the complex anatomy of the liver, pancreas, and biliary system. These surgeries require a high degree of technical skill and expertise.

Dr. Anvesh possesses specialized training in these complex procedures, utilizing both open and minimally invasive techniques to achieve the best possible outcomes for his patients.

Recognizing the Signs

Symptoms of HPB cancers can be non-specific, making early diagnosis difficult.

Key Symptoms

Jaundice (yellowing of the skin and eyes).
Abdominal pain, particularly in the upper right side.
Unexplained weight loss and loss of appetite.
Dark urine and pale, greasy stools.
Nausea, vomiting, and persistent fatigue.

Surgical Procedures

Dr. Anvesh performs some of the most complex abdominal surgeries for HPB cancers.

Whipple Procedure (Pancreaticoduodenectomy)

A complex surgery to remove tumors in the head of the pancreas.

Liver Resection (Hepatectomy)

Surgical removal of a portion of the liver to treat liver cancer.

The Surgical Journey

Navigating complex HPB surgery with expert guidance and support.

Advanced Imaging & Diagnosis

Utilizing high-resolution CT, MRI, and ERCP to precisely map the tumor and surrounding structures.

Multidisciplinary Tumor Board

A team of HPB surgeons, oncologists, and interventional radiologists reviews each case to determine the optimal treatment strategy.

Complex Surgical Resection

Performing intricate procedures like the Whipple operation or liver resection with a focus on complete tumor removal and safety.

Intensive Post-Operative Care

Specialized ICU and ward care to manage recovery, including nutritional and pancreatic enzyme support.

Frequently Asked Questions

The Whipple procedure (pancreaticoduodenectomy) is a complex operation to remove the head of the pancreas, the first part of the small intestine (duodenum), the gallbladder, and the bile duct. It is a major surgery for pancreatic cancer that Dr. Anvesh performs with high precision.

Liver resection is a major surgery but is safe when performed by an experienced HPB surgeon. The liver has a unique ability to regenerate. Dr. Anvesh uses advanced techniques to maximize safety and preserve as much healthy liver tissue as possible.

Recovery is intensive and requires a hospital stay, often including time in the ICU. Full recovery can take several weeks to months. Dr. Anvesh and his team provide comprehensive post-operative support to manage pain and nutrition.
